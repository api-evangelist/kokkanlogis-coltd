KokkanLogis Co.,Ltd. (주식회사 곳간, branded **KOKKAN** / 도심창고 곳간) is a South Korean
urban self-storage and logistics operator headquartered in Gangnam-gu, Seoul. It rents
managed storage units in five sizes (S through XXL) across 22 branches in Seoul, Busan,
Incheon, Gyeonggi and Chungbuk, serving both households and businesses. Customers pick a
branch and unit size, pay, and manage their contract through the KOKKAN iOS and Android
apps, with recurring monthly card billing, long-term and promotional discounting, an
address-rental product, and an optional pickup service. The company also runs a franchise
and partnership program.

Surfaced through the 500 Global portfolio.

- Website: https://kokkan.co.kr
- Corporate site: https://www.kokkanlogis.co.kr (access-restricted from outside Korea)
- Branches: https://kokkan.co.kr/branch_list.php
- Blog: https://blog.naver.com/kokkan
